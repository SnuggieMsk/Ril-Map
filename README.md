# Global Stocks Investing Dossier

A clickable, audit-grade knowledge dossier on direct global stock investing from India.

## What's covered

- **The two routes**: LRS (Liberalised Remittance Scheme) and GIFT City IFSC — full comparison across 18 dimensions
- **LRS deep dive**: limits, TCS rules (FY 25-26), 7-step process, permitted/prohibited uses, edge cases, RBI purpose codes
- **GIFT City IFSC**: NSE IFSC vs India INX, Section 10(4D) tax nuance, funding routes
- **ICICI Direct**: both pathways (LRS/IBKR + GIFT City IFSC), pricing, pros/cons, 10-step starting checklist, end-to-end cost waterfall
- **US market**: exchanges, hours, instruments, US tax-at-source (incl. the **estate tax landmine**), W-8BEN, mitigation strategies, curated stock + ETF + UCITS picks
- **18 country regulatory profiles** on an interactive Leaflet world map
- **Indian taxation**: capital gains (post Budget 2024), dividends, Schedule FA, Black Money Act
- **12 risk categories** with severity & mitigation
- **5 live calculators**: LRS budget, TCS estimator, capital gains, dividend WHT/DTAA, US estate tax exposure
- **63 FAQs** across 10 categories with search + filter
- **56-term glossary** with search
- **16 source references** to operative regulations

## Tech

Pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript — no build step. Leaflet for the world map. Chart.js loaded for future charts.
